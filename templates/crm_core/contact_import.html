Upload a CSV file to add multiple contacts and link them to companies.

Download sample CSV
File Guidelines
  • Accepted format: .csv files only, up to 2 MB and 5,000 rows.
  • Required columns: name.
  • Optional columns: email, phone, job_title, company, status.
  • Company matching: Matched case-insensitively within your business. If a company doesn't exist, it will be automatically created!
  • Status: Allowed values are Lead, Customer, or Inactive (defaults to Lead).
  • Duplicates: Rows matching an existing contact email in your business will be skipped.
  • Owner: Imported contacts will be assigned to you as the owner.
