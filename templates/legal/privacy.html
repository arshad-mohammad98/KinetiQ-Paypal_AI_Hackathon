Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for KinetiQ, we collect your business name, your name, and your email address to authenticate your workspace.

2. How We Protect Your Data

All business data is isolated at the database level. Each business record is strictly scoped to your organization ID. We enforce HTTPS-only cookies and automated isolation testing.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your data or wish to export or delete your business records, please contact your workspace administrator.
